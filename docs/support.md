# Fluffy Merge Support

Thanks for playing Fluffy Merge! If something isn't working, or you have
an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** [thennmininaturals@icloud.com](mailto:thennmininaturals@icloud.com)

Please include your device model, its iOS or Android version, and what
happened. We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Drag to aim, then release to drop a puff. Two puffs of the same kind
merge into a bigger one. Don't let the jar overflow past the dotted line!
In the app, tap the 🎓 button on the home screen for **How to Play**.

**I'm stuck. What do power-ups do?**
- **Sweep** clears the smallest puffs.
- **Pop** removes one puff you pick.
- **Grow** levels one puff up.
- **Shake** loosens the pile.

You earn more by reaching new levels in a run, from daily challenges, or
in the Shop.

**My purchase didn't arrive.**
Close and reopen the app: purchases that finished in the background are
delivered on launch. If the coins still aren't there, email us with the
date of purchase. On iPhone, you can also request a refund through Apple
at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Will I lose my progress if I change phones?**
Progress is saved on your device. Restoring a device backup (iCloud or
Google) to your new phone brings it with it.

**Does the game collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy).

**Sound or vibration is too much.**
Open **Settings** in the app to turn music, sound effects or haptics off,
change the volume, or reduce animations.
